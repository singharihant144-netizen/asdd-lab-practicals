[ WIRELESS LAB ]

Three signals.
Three stories.

Each experiment includes the complete submitted code, one-click copy, a Python download, and focused viva questions in the Viva Prep section.

01 / MOBILITY

Handover

Compare two base-station signal strengths as a mobile moves and identify the handover point.

02 / CHANNEL

Multipath effect

Combine delayed and attenuated copies of a transmitted waveform to observe channel distortion.

03 / DIGITAL

BPSK + AWGN

Transmit random bits with BPSK, add Gaussian noise, detect bits, and calculate BER.

No external database was included in the supplied WMC folder. These simulations generate their signals and bits directly in Python. Install NumPy and Matplotlib before running.
